Studio blurb (long)

Cool and Unusual Studio is an indie studio making games and stories for grown-ups who never stopped playing. Creator Fly is the most visible face behind the work, but the studio comes first. As he puts it, "I'm the main cog, but still a cog in something greater."

The studio is currently developing two connected projects. Project Awesome Mobile (PAM, a working title) is a multi-genre 2.5D avatar hub game for mobile and PC. It features an HD-2D look, with pixel sprites in a lit 3D world, plus pets, housing and an in-game currency called Bucks. It is ad-free with opt-in ads only, and everything for sale can also be earned in-game.

CUTTS, the Cool & Unusual Table Top System, is a tabletop RPG system in development, with a closed beta coming. Both projects share the big city of Nexus. Cool & Unusual is an 18+ community.

Project blurb: Project Awesome Mobile (PAM)

Project Awesome Mobile (working title) is a multi-genre 2.5D avatar hub game for mobile and PC, set in Nexus, the city from CUTTS. Its HD-2D look puts pixel sprites in a lit 3D world. Players build avatars, keep pets, set up housing and earn Bucks across hubs including The Wreckyard (siege), The Sorting House (match-3) and Lantern Row Underworks (classic side-scroller). It is ad-free with opt-in ads only. Everything for sale is earnable in-game at a fair grind, with no energy limits, no gambling, and loot boxes that are earned, never sold. Solo-friendly and community-first, with teams of 2 to 3 and neighborhoods of up to 12 players. In development.

Project blurb: CUTTS

CUTTS, the Cool & Unusual Table Top System, is a tabletop roleplaying game system in development, with a closed beta coming. It comes with a light core setting: the big city of Nexus, plus a few pocket scenarios for shorter adventures. Run it in any world you like; Nexus is just a place to start. Nexus is also the setting for Project Awesome Mobile.
