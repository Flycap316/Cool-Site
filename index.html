About the studio

We're Cool & Unusual, an indie studio that makes things we'd want to play ourselves: games and stories with heart, a little mischief and a lot of personality.

The studio is the name on the door. Fly is the guy you'll see most often, building, tinkering and sharing what's next. In his words:

"I'm the main cog, but still a cog in something greater."

That something greater is the studio, and everyone who plays along with us.

What we're working on

Project Awesome Mobile (PAM)

A 2.5D avatar hub game for mobile and PC, set in the city of Nexus. Pixel sprites, a lit 3D world, pets, housing and a bunch of games under one roof. Fair to play, built for friends. In development.
