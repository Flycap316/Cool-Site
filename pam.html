Project Awesome Mobile

Working name. The real title is coming later.

Mobile + PC18+

Status: In development. No release dates yet, and we won't promise any until we're sure.

What is it?

PAM is a multi-genre 2.5D avatar hub game for mobile and PC. It's set in Nexus, the big city from our tabletop system, CUTTS.

It's got an HD-2D look: pixel sprites living in a lit 3D world. Think classic charm with modern lighting.

What you'll do

  • Make yourself: Build your avatar, then dress them up at the clothing shop.
  • Settle in: Get a place to call home, and a pet or two to share it with.
  • Earn Bucks: Bucks are the in-game currency. Play, earn, spend.
  • Play a lot of different games: Each hub is its own kind of game.

First-playable hubs

  • The Wreckyard: A siege game. Knock things down, strategically.
  • The Sorting House: Match-3, Nexus style.
  • Lantern Row Underworks: A classic side-scroller, down below the city.
  • Clothing shop: Look good. Look weird. Look like you.
  • Avatar creator: Where it all starts.

Better with friends (but fine on your own)

PAM is solo-friendly and community-first. Play on your own anytime, or team up:

  • Teams of 2 to 3 for playing together.
  • Neighborhoods of up to 12 players to live alongside.
  • Link is how you connect. Share your Link, and your friends can find you and play with you.

Our fair-play promise

We've drawn some hard lines, and we're sticking to them:

  • Ad-free. Ads are opt-in only. You'll only see one if you choose to.
  • Everything for sale is also earnable in-game at a fair grind.
  • No energy limits. Play as long as you like.
  • No gambling.
  • Loot boxes are earned only, never sold.
