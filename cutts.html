CUTTS is our tabletop roleplaying game system. It's in development, with a closed beta coming. It's growing, and it's still taking shape.

Where it's set

CUTTS comes with a light core setting, so you get a starting point without a 400-page homework assignment:

  • Nexus: A big city with plenty of room for trouble, adventure and the occasional bad decision.
  • Pocket scenarios: A few smaller, self-contained settings for when your group wants a quick change of scenery.

Run it in any world you like. Nexus is just a place to start.

Nexus is shared ground

Nexus is also where Project Awesome Mobile takes place. Same city, different ways to explore it.
